## How the comparison was run

- Every detector received the same saved prefix of up to 160 GPT-2 tokens per passage. Each prefix fit a single eligible CanonWright scoring window. CanonWright 0.2.28 scored all eligible windows in follow-up mode at threshold zero to expose both raw scores. We reconstructed its shipped decisions at Desklib ≥ 0.80 and GPT-2 discrepancy ≥ 1.5. Quick matches are all retained in the current report, including matches that lack corroboration.
- Binoculars ran locally on two RTX 3090s, one BF16 Falcon-7B model per card. We used the authors' scoring functions, the base/instruct model pair, the native Transformers Falcon implementation, and their published low-false-positive cutoff of score < 0.8536432310785527. We did not tune the cutoff on the evaluation data. [Binoculars implementation](https://github.com/ahans30/Binoculars/tree/c8ae2f90d50ee696418bc71d8d9e5020e5f9d7b8)
- For standard Ghostbuster we replayed the authors' archived Ada and Davinci log probabilities locally, checked that both streams matched the exact input bytes, then applied the published Brown-corpus n-grams, feature expressions, normalization and calibrated classifier weights. The default binary cutoff was 0.5. All 64 archival prefixes aligned. These are samples from the authors' benchmark corpus, and its human material overlaps the source used to fit their released classifier. The replay is a compatibility result, not an independent validation of the released model. Fresh passages have no archived provider probabilities. The published standalone classifier cannot score those passages entirely offline. [Reference classifier](https://github.com/vivek3141/ghostbuster/blob/f60068d006b7dc617af90e7148118837e85d0555/classify.py) · [Archived data](https://github.com/vivek3141/ghostbuster-data/tree/86ebd72590556a81622986fab736ab9227a948af)
- For a deployable offline comparison we used Ghostbuster's nine published n-gram symbolic features and its Brown-corpus trigram method. We fitted a standard scaler and logistic regression on separate WritingPrompts IDs 1–200 for each of the human and GPT classes, checking that none of the evaluation texts was an exact duplicate. We used a fixed 0.5 threshold. This is a **local refit**. Even the upstream experiment titled “N-Gram Only” concatenates the symbolic features with handcrafted provider features in its training script. [Upstream experiment](https://github.com/vivek3141/ghostbuster/blob/f60068d006b7dc617af90e7148118837e85d0555/run.py)
- Fiction controls comprised 32 WritingPrompts human stories and 32 archived generated stories (16 GPT alternate-prompt and 16 Claude; source IDs 501–532), plus 16 human excerpts from four novels by Austen, Stoker, Wells and Dickens and 16 new style-conditioned generations. Rulebook controls comprised 16 human 2014 SRD rules sections and 16 spell descriptions, 32 locally generated topic-matched entries, 16 human excerpts from Dungeon World and 16 new style-conditioned generations. Prompted generations used Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct and SmolLM2-1.7B-Instruct in equal numbers. The style-conditioned prompts asked for a new passage on a different topic using a human excerpt as a style example. [SRD data](https://github.com/5e-bits/5e-database/tree/a6212beb4b278917c2cff41c2b04c4ef4f3e0d6f/src/2014/en) · [Dungeon World source](https://github.com/Sagelt/Dungeon-World/tree/e67bd51c09d24518a7f989149b76094fbcc7fecc/text)
- A separate challenge asked the two local models to revise 16 human passages per domain for clarity while preserving voice and meaning. Two rulebook excerpts returned the same word sequence and were excluded from the changed-text counts. The CSV retains those rows as unchanged copies. Revisions are reported separately from fully generated prose. Multiple excerpts share books, prompts, or source passages. The observed rates describe these samples and do not estimate a population error rate.

## Verification in the application

The product scorer replayed all 224 recorded inputs, including the two unchanged copies. The conditional Binoculars stage scored 64 eligible inputs and promoted 14. Its binary decisions matched the reference implementation on every eligible input. The largest observed absolute difference in the ratio was less than 0.00000006. Pagination reused all 64 cached scores. A separate empty-cache test exercised all three models together on the installed GPUs, including a below-0.80 passage that standalone Binoculars would have flagged. That passage did not enter the optional stage.
