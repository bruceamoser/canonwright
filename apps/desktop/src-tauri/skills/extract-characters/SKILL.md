---
name: extract-characters
description: Extract named characters, aliases, relationships, and motivations.
---
Identify named or recurring figures with a distinct role. Record explicit names and aliases, relevant relationships, current goals, and possible motivations. State whether a motivation is expressed, attributed by another character, or inferred from behavior. Ask whether aliases refer to one person only when the same identity is plausible and the decision matters. Skip incidental unnamed figures.
