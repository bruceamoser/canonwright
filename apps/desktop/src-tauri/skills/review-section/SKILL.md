description: Review a selected passage according to the book type.
---
For a novel, examine viewpoint, causal continuity, and character agency. For a core rulebook, walk through the procedure, checking actors, triggers, outcomes, and whether examples match the written rule. For a content book, look at usability at the table, clue access, and audience boundaries. Prioritize a few consequential findings, with evidence and a practical revision question or suggestion. Avoid converting preferences into factual errors.
